FOREVER & ALWAYS — YOUR WEDDING PLANNER
=======================================

TO TEST ON YOUR WINDOWS COMPUTER:
1. Install Node.js 22+ from https://nodejs.org/ (if needed).
2. Double-click START-WEDDING-PLANNER.bat.
3. Visit http://localhost:3000
4. Enter the password: STWed2027
5. Try adding wedding planners, tasks, budgets and inspiration photos.

The app starts in LOCAL TEST MODE with sample tasks and budget items.
These are not shared with anyone. No external accounts are required for a preview.

TO USE IT WITH YOUR TEAM ON VERCEL:
1. Follow google-apps-script/SETUP.md to authorize saving to your Google Drive.
2. Follow section C of README.md to deploy to Vercel and set environment variables.
3. Confirm it says 'Google Drive connected', then share your Vercel URL and
   the wedding website password privately with your planners.

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ihLbsZeSl8hZ37Bec7mijpX6zkqr2IC4
